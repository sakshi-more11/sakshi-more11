## 🧠 About Me

I'm a final-stage AI/ML undergraduate specializing in **Large Language Models (LLMs), Generative AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Natural Language Processing (NLP)** — with academic training backed by a 9.11 CGPA and a published research paper in the space.

My core interest lies in the reliability gap in generative AI: language models are powerful, but they hallucinate, and most systems aren't built to catch that. My published research on confidence-calibrated RAG verification is a direct response to that problem, and it's the lens I bring to every system I design — grounding, evaluation, and trust, not just fluent output.

I approach AI work the way I'd approach any engineering discipline: read the research, understand the failure modes, then build something that holds up under real conditions. I'm currently seeking an internship where I can apply this thinking to production-scale LLM, RAG, or NLP systems.
